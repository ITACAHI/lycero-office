# Freelance Invoice VN -- Hoa Don Dich Vu Tu Xuat

## 1. Doi tuong ap dung

Ca nhan cu tru va khong cu tru co thu nhap tu dich vu (freelance, tu van, thiet ke, lap trinh...) phai xuat hoa don GTGT hoac hoa don ban hang khi co yeu cau tu khach hang doanh nghiep.

## 2. Cac loai hoa don

### 2.1. Hoa don gia tri gia tang (GTGT)
- **Doi tuong:** Ca nhan da dang ky thue va co ma so thue (MST).
- **Thue suat:** 10% tren gia tri dich vu.
- **Phuong phap:** Khau tru (doanh nghiep khau tru) hoac truc tiep (ca nhan tu ke khai).

### 2.2. Hoa don ban hang
- **Doi tuong:** Ca nhan/ho kinh doanh chua dang ky thue GTGT.
- **Khong chiu thue GTGT**, nhung thu nhap van chiu thue TNCN.

### 2.3. Chung tu thu (bien lai)
- Su dung khi khach hang la ca nhan khong yeu cau hoa don.
- Tuy nhien, de dam bao quyen loi cho ca hai ben, **uu tien xuat hoa don**.

## 3. Thue TNCN cho Freelancer

### 3.1. Can cu phap ly
- **Luat Thue TNCN so 04/2007/QH12** (sua doi 2012, 2014)
- **Dieu 2:** Doi tuong nop thue: ca nhan cu tru co thu nhap tu kinh doanh
- **Dieu 10:** Thu nhap chiu thue tu kinh doanh, dich vu
- **Dieu 23:** Giam tru gia canh (nguoi phu thuoc)

### 3.2. Bieu thue luy tien tung phan (ap dung tu 01/07/2020)

| Bac | Thu nhap tinh thue/nam (VND) | Thue suat |
|------|------------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Den 60.000.000 | 5% |
| 2 | Tren 60.000.000 den 120.000.000 | 10% |
| 3 | Tren 120.000.000 den 216.000.000 | 15% |
| 4 | Tren 216.000.000 den 384.000.000 | 20% |
| 5 | Tren 384.000.000 den 624.000.000 | 25% |
| 6 | Tren 624.000.000 den 960.000.000 | 30% |
| 7 | Tren 960.000.000 | 35% |

### 3.3. Giam tru gia canh
- **Ban than:** 11.000.000 d/thang = 132.000.000 d/nam
- **Nguoi phu thuoc:** 4.400.000 d/thang/nguoi

### 3.4. Cong thuc tinh thue TNCN

 Thu nhap tinh thue = Tong thu nhap - Giam tru ban than - Giam tru nguoi phu thuoc - Chi phi thuc te
 Chi phi thuc te = 50% tong thu nhap (doi voi ca nhan khong co MST, khong truc tiep hach toan)

## 4. Cau truc hoa don dich vu tu xuat

### 4.1. Thong tin bat buoc tren hoa don

 (Ky, ghi ro ho ten)      (Ky, ghi ro ho ten)    (Ky, dong dau)

### 4.2. Huong dan dien

1. **Ten hang hoa, dich vu:** Mo ta ro rang, cu the cong viec da thuc hien.
2. **Don vi tinh:** Tuy theo tinh chat cong viec (gio cong, du an, thang, bai viet...).
3. **Don gia:** Gia chua co thue GTGT (gia NET).
4. **Thue GTGT:** Ap dung 10% neu ca nhan co MST va dang ky phuong phap khau tru.

## 5. Quy trinh xuat hoa don cho Freelancer

 Buoc 1: Ky hop dong dich vu -> Xac dinh gia tri, thoi han
 Buoc 2: Hoan thanh cong viec -> Ban giao san pham, nghiep thu
 Buoc 3: Xuat hoa don -> Theo dung dinh dang quy dinh
 Buoc 4: Gui hoa don cho khach hang -> Qua email, hoac ban cung
 Buoc 5: Theo doi thanh toan -> Danh dau da thanh toan trong so
 Buoc 6: Ke khai thue (neu co MST) -> Hang quy, nop TNCN cuoi nam

## 6. Checklist truoc khi xuat hoa don

- [ ] Hop dong dich vu da duoc ky
- [ ] Cong viec da hoan thanh va duoc nghiep thu
- [ ] Thong tin nguoi ban day du (ten, MST, dia chi, STK)
- [ ] Thong tin nguoi mua day du (ten, MST neu co, dia chi)
- [ ] Mo ta dich vu ro rang, khong gay nham lan
- [ ] Tinh dung thue GTGT (neu ap dung)
- [ ] So tien bang chu viet dung (theo quy tac viet so tien VND)
- [ ] Da luu ban sao vao ho so ca nhan
- [ ] Da gui cho khach hang va theo doi thanh toan
